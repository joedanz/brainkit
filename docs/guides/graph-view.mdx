Every compiled vault is a web of notes joined by `[[wikilinks]]`. There are two
ways to see that web as an interactive graph, depending on how you use your
vault.

[Typed relations](/concepts/retrieval#typed-relations) (`up`, `down`, `same`,
`prev`, `next`) are ordinary frontmatter wikilinks, so they draw as edges in
both views with no extra setup — the graph you see already includes them.

## The dashboard's Graph tab

The [live dashboard](/guides/reference-deployment) has a **Graph** tab: your
notes as points colored by space, linked where notes reference each other. In
the light theme it reads as a territory map — each space a tinted region with
its name at the centre; in the dark theme it is a constellation, each space a
glow. It works for everyone, including people who only ever talk to their
assistant in chat, since the dashboard is just a URL.

- **Space names** float at each cluster's centre, always on. The **legend**
  (bottom-left) lists every space with its page count; tap a chip to hide or
  show that space. The dashed **orphans** chip rings pages nothing links to.
- **Labels** (in Settings) choose how many notes are named: *hubs* (the 12
  most linked-to), *more* (about 25), or *all* — and names never overlap,
  because a name is only drawn when there is room for it at the current zoom.
  Phones start on *hubs*, desktops on *more*; the choice is remembered.
- **Hover** a note to see its full path. **Click** (or tap) a note to focus
  it: its neighbours stay bright and everything else fades. Click the empty
  canvas to clear. The node card lists what it links to, what links back,
  its facts, and opens it in the Query tab. **Search** focuses the first
  match, so any page is reachable without hover.
- **Size** is inbound links: the more notes cite a page, the bigger it draws.
- **3D** renders the same graph as a constellation you can orbit; **Fit**
  reframes the whole graph in either mode. **Full graph** appears when a
  large vault was cut to its 300 most-connected notes.
- **Settings** also mark dead ends (pages that link nowhere) and tune node
  size, link width, and the layout forces. Everything sticks across visits.

The graph updates live: add a note or a link and it springs into place without
losing your zoom or layout. On a phone the legend becomes a scrolling row above
the canvas and the node card slides up from the bottom; one finger orbits or
pans, a pinch zooms.

## Obsidian's graph view

If you work with a cloned copy of your vault
([per-employee setup](/guides/per-employee-setup)), the vault is a plain
Obsidian vault — open it in Obsidian and its built-in graph view works with no
configuration, alongside backlinks, canvas, and every other Obsidian feature.

## The graph you see is the graph you're allowed to see

Both views draw from your compiled vault, and the
[compiler](/concepts/the-compiler) stubs out links that point at notes outside
your permissions — a note you can't read is never on your machine, so it can't
appear as a node or an edge. The graph isn't just a pretty view of your
knowledge; it's an exact picture of your slice of the company brain.
