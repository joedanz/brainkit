The [live dashboard](/guides/reference-deployment)'s **Pages** tab shows the
vault the way it is laid out on disk: spaces and their folders, each with a
page count, and every page you are allowed to read.

## Desktop

Two panes. The left tree (280px) lists folders with their page counts —
click one to open or close it; the folder of the page you are reading stays
expanded and the page is highlighted. The right pane is the reader: the note
rendered as Markdown with live `[[wikilinks]]`, a raw view, and **Linked
from** / **Links to** under the body. Picking a page swaps only the reader,
so the tree keeps its scroll and its open folders.

## Phone

Below 900px the tree gives way to a breadcrumb listing: folders first, then
pages, each sorted alphabetically. Tap a page to read it full width; the
crumb at the top goes back.

## Deep links

Every open page has an address: `#page=<path>` (for example
`#page=Company%2FHome.md`). Copy the URL to cite the page; the dashboard opens
it in the Pages tab with its folder expanded. The Query tab's `#note=` links
still work and open there.

## What you see is what you may read

The tree is built from the vault's index, not from the filesystem, and the
index holds exactly the notes the [compiler](/concepts/the-compiler) gave your
slice. A page outside your permissions is not in your vault, not in your
index, and not in the tree. If the tree is empty, the vault has not been
indexed yet — run `brain index`.
